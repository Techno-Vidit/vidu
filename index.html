01 / The picture

A material that outlives its moment.

Plastic production more than doubled between 2000 and 2019. Without stronger action, it could nearly triple by 2060.

Plastic is woven into daily life, from food packaging to medical equipment. The problem is not simply that plastic exists: it is that so much is designed to be used briefly, while the waste persists and spreads.

Most plastic is made from fossil fuels. As production rises, so do emissions and the pressure on waste systems that already struggle to keep up. Recycling helps, but it cannot keep pace with ever-growing production.

“The most effective waste is the waste we never create.”

03 / Turn awareness into action

Less plastic starts upstream.

Personal habits matter. So do the choices made by businesses and governments. Both can move the needle.

01

Choose reuse where it works

Carry a refillable bottle or cup, and choose durable options when they are practical and accessible.

↗
02

Ask for better systems

Support local refill, repair, and waste-reduction programs. Ask brands how they plan to reduce unnecessary packaging.

↗
03

Back policies that prevent waste

Look for policies that reduce avoidable single-use products and make producers responsible for the waste they create.
